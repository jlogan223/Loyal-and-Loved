🎆 Fireworks and dark evenings

Quick autumn hints

A few fast wins for Bonfire Night and the darker months — tap through for the full guide.

🎆 Get ahead of the fireworks

Fireworks run for weeks either side of 5 November. A little preparation makes the noisy nights far easier for dogs and cats.

  • Walk dogs in daylight, and bring cats in before dusk with food, water and a litter tray inside.
  • Close curtains, put the radio or TV on, and set up a quiet den they can choose to hide in.
  • Plug in a calming diffuser a few days early, such as Adaptil for dogs on Amazon.

🔦 Be seen on dark walks

With the clocks back, most weekday walks now happen in the dark. Drivers see a reflective dog far sooner than one in a plain collar.

  • Add a light or reflective layer to your dog as well as yourself.
  • Keep your dog on the lead near roads after dark, even if their recall is good.
  • A rechargeable LED dog collar is cheap and easy to see from a distance.
