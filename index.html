🎄 Christmas and winter

Quick winter hints

A few fast wins for the cold, dark weeks and the run-up to Christmas — tap through for the full guide.

🍫 Keep the Christmas food out of reach

Chocolate, grapes, raisins, mince pies and Christmas pudding are all dangerous to dogs, and a busy kitchen makes it easy for something to fall on the floor.

  • Keep selection boxes and tree chocolates high up, not under the tree.
  • Ask visitors not to feed the dog from the table.
  • Save your vet's out-of-hours number in your phone before the holidays.

❄️ Cold, wet walks

Short-coated, small and older dogs feel the cold quickly, and gritted pavements are hard on paws.

  • A waterproof coat helps thin-coated and older dogs on long winter walks.
  • Wipe paws after walks on gritted roads, and check between the pads.
  • Towel-dry properly when you get home, or try a dog drying coat.
